Customer Intelligence and Churn Platform

A full customer-analytics system for a subscription business. Cleaned data sits in MySQL, 37 SQL business questions answer the common management questions, and three models cover churn, customer segments and lifetime value, all shown in a 7-page Streamlit dashboard. An AI copilot answers plain-English questions with checked, read-only SQL, summarises customer feedback and suggests retention actions.

In a simulated retention campaign with stated costs, contacting customers by model ranking beat contacting everyone by about 54%. The churn signal is modest (ROC-AUC 0.62).

Predicts which bank customers are likely to leave so a team can contact them first. The model was tuned for recall because missing a leaving customer costs more than a false alarm. It runs behind a REST API with a simple web screen for non-technical users.

Loan Default Risk Scoring

Scores how likely a loan applicant is to default, so an underwriter can see risk at a glance. Customer, credit-bureau, loan and transaction data was loaded into a MySQL database, analysed with 10 SQL queries, and used to train and compare four models. The output is a 0 to 100 risk score (Low, Medium, High) in a Streamlit dashboard with filters and a high-risk watchlist.

In testing, loans in the High group defaulted about 46% of the time, against about 10% in the Low group (ROC-AUC 0.72 on held-out data).
